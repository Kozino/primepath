Privacy Policy (Summary)

Prime Path Travels & Tours Limited collects only the personal data required to process travel bookings (names, passport details, contact information, payment confirmations), in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

Data is used solely for issuing tickets, embassy/visa submissions, supplier bookings and communicating about your trip. Passenger data required for ticketing is shared with airlines, GDS providers and relevant authorities as necessary. Card details are processed by PCI-DSS certified gateways and never stored by us.

You may request access, correction or deletion of your data at any time via info@primepathtravels.com. Full policy published at launch.

Terms of Service (Summary)

Fares are subject to airline availability and only guaranteed once ticketed. Ticket validity, changes, baggage allowances and cancellations follow the fare rules of the operating airline.

Prime Path acts as an agent of airlines and suppliers. Service fees, where applicable, are shown before payment. Visa issuance is at the sole discretion of embassies — we assist with documentation only.

Bookings are subject to our full terms (available on request) and applicable NCAA regulations and Nigerian law. Full terms published at launch.

Refund Policy (Summary)

Refunds follow airline fare rules. Where a fare is refundable, we process within 14 working days of airline approval, less airline penalties and a handling fee shown upfront.

Non-refundable fares may still qualify for partial refunds of unused taxes. Airline-initiated cancellations are refunded in full where permitted. Visa service fees are non-refundable once documentation has been submitted.

Please contact us before initiating a card chargeback — we resolve disputes faster. Full policy published at launch.
